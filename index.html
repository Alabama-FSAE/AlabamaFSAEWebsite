Crimson Racing is the University of Alabama's Formula SAE team. Every season, a student-run engineering organization designs, manufactures, and races a formula-style car from a blank sheet of paper — competing against nearly 100 collegiate teams from around the world.

What we're about

Development

Real engineering, start to finish

Every member works through the full design cycle — concept, analysis, manufacturing, and validation — on a real vehicle, not a simulation.

Teamwork

Aero, chassis, electrical, powertrain, suspension, and more all have to work as one system by competition day.

Design

Defend every decision

Judges at competition interrogate our design choices directly — every part on the car needs a reason to be there.

Competition

We compete head-to-head against top collegiate engineering programs at Formula SAE events each year.
